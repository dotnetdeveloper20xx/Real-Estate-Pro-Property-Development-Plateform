# Legal & Compliance Module — Documentation

## Overview

The Legal & Compliance Module (Module 3) protects BuildEstate Pro legally and operationally by managing contracts, legal documents, compliance requirements, insurance records, regulatory evidence, and audit history. It is cross-cutting — supporting all other modules by providing the legal framework that governs land acquisition, planning approvals, construction, and sales.

## Quick Start

1. Navigate to **Legal & Compliance** from the main menu
2. The **Dashboard** gives you an instant overview of all legal KPIs
3. Use the sidebar to access Legal Cases, Contracts, Compliance, Insurance, or Audit Records
4. Create your first Legal Case to begin tracking legal matters

## Key Roles

- **Legal & Compliance Officer** — Full access to all features
- **Finance Director** — Contract approval authority for high-value agreements
- **Acquisition Manager** — Read access to cases linked to their opportunities
- **Admin/Support** — Data entry, document uploads, case creation support
